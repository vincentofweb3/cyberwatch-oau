# Research Notes and Evidence

Research date: 2026-11-06

## OAU-specific evidence

1. [OAU Cybersecurity Tips](https://oauife.edu.ng/cybersecurity-tips/) provides static preventive guidance, including strong passwords, two-factor authentication, safe browsing, updates, backups, and caution on public networks.
2. [OAU Security Summit](https://oauife.edu.ng/oau-holds-a-2-day-security-summit-titled-strengthening-security-at-obafemi-awolowo-university-challenges-opportunities-and-strategic-solutions/) explicitly identified cybersecurity implementation, intelligence-based threat response, awareness, and resilience as university needs.
3. OAU has repeatedly published scam warnings, including [Beware of Scammers](https://oauife.edu.ng/press-release-beware-of-scammers-oau-does-not-recruit-give-contract-via-social-media/), [mail and social-media scammers](https://oauife.edu.ng/beware-of-mail-and-social-media-scammers/), and an [online scammers disclaimer](https://oauife.edu.ng/disclaimer-in-respect-of-online-scammers-2/).
4. The [Faculty of Computing Science and Engineering](https://oauife.edu.ng/faculty-of-computing-science-and-engineering/) lists Cybersecurity as a research area and identifies the Department of Computer Science and Cyber Security and the CySe Lab.
5. OAU already operates an [e-portal](https://eportal.oauife.edu.ng/) and [transcript processing system](https://tps.oauife.edu.ng/). CyberWatch therefore avoids rebuilding central registration, results, or transcript functionality.
6. The [2026 NITDA Innovation Hub announcement](https://oauife.edu.ng/oau65-tijani-commissions-nitda-innovation-hub-urges-students-to-build-solutions-for-global-market/) emphasizes practical, technology-driven solutions rather than certificate-only work.

## Standards and engineering references

1. [NIST SP 800-61 Rev. 3](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/61/r3/final), published April 2025, integrates incident response across cybersecurity risk-management activities.
2. [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) supplies the Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover structure.
3. [FIRST Traffic Light Protocol 2.0](https://www.first.org/tlp/) defines sharing labels used in the application.
4. [OWASP ASVS](https://owasp.org/www-project-application-security-verification-standard/) and OWASP cheat sheets inform validation, session, authorization, IDOR, and logging controls.
5. Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 principles inform data minimization, purpose limitation, confidentiality, accountability, and retention. Legal interpretation is outside this project's scope.

## Research conclusion

The public evidence supports a product that connects awareness with structured incident handling. OAU has guidance and warning posts, but the publicly visible journey does not provide a unified report-and-track workflow. This is a public-surface observation, not proof that OAU has no internal incident system. Before deployment, the department must validate the workflow with the Computer Centre, CySe Lab, Corporate Affairs, Legal/Data Protection personnel, and campus security.
